What did everyone find?

People recorded thousands of different living things.

The Canopy of Life

Each block is one taxon ID. Large blocks were recorded often. Small blocks may come from a single sighting. Together, they show the range of life people noticed.

Block size shows the number of sightings, not how common something is in the forest. The available taxonomy is simplified into a broad group, a genus level name and then a taxon name.

How often was each taxon seen?

Most were seen only once or twice.

A few taxa appear again and again. Many appear in just one or two records.

A taxon seen once may not be rare. It may simply be hard to find, or someone may have looked only once.

Here, “taxon” means one distinct taxon ID in the export. It does not always mean a species.

Behind every dot

Every dot began when someone met a living thing.

A sighting may look like one small mark on a screen. It began with a real encounter: a person stopped, looked, took a photo and shared what they found.

These photographs come from iNaturalist records that allow reuse. Credits and licenses are shown with each image.
